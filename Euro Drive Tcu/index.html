Wet clutch (oil bath)

The multi-plate clutch discs are submerged in oil. The oil ensures cooling, lubrication, and clamping pressure across the clutch pack.

  • ✅ Handles massive torque from 350 Nm to 1000 Nm.
  • ✅ Excellent thermal management and increased reliability.
  • ⚠️ Requires strict oil changes every 60,000 km.
Street driving should target low slip and clean overlap. Track use increases heat load, especially when the oil condition is compromised.

Maintenance & oil strategy

Racing and street drivability guidance for DSG and S tronic oil quality, clutch health, and thermal fatigue.

Wet clutch maintenance

DSG gearbox oil undergoes extreme shear and temperature stress. Worn oil loses friction properties, leading to slipping, overheating, and clutch damage.

  • Street cars: oil changes every 60,000 km or sooner if the vehicle is used aggressively.
  • Track or repeated hard launches: shorten the interval and inspect thermal load during log review.
  • Do not mix fluid specifications — mismatch can create pressure drift and clutch wear.

DL501 special attention

Unlike the DQ250 / DQ500, the DL501 has two separate oil circuits that must be treated independently.

  • Hydraulic compartment: approx. 7.5L ATF, lubricates clutches and mechatronic units.
  • Mechanical compartment: approx. 4.5L MTF, lubricates gears, front differential, and transfer case.
  • Check both circuits when the customer reports long-term hesitation or thermal drift.
